✳ THE BASICS

What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) compares your weight with your height to provide a general screening measure for adults.

WEIGHT (KG) HEIGHT (M) × HEIGHT (M)

Adult BMI categories

Standard adult screening ranges

Underweight Below 18.5
Normal weight 18.5–24.9
Overweight 25–29.9
Obesity range 30 or above
